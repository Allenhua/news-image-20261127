1. **Drone attack in Kuwait kills two Italians (LEAD)** — Two Italians
   were killed in an Iranian drone attack in Kuwait on Thursday, Italy's
   foreign minister Antonio Tajani told the Senate: the victims were
   technicians for Rheinmetall Italia, the Italian unit of German defence
   group Rheinmetall, working at an air-defence surveillance station when
   it was targeted. Kuwait's Defence Ministry confirmed the two died of
   their injuries, after first reporting four people wounded by falling
   debris when a "vital facility" was targeted in what it described as an
   Iranian attack; a Kuwaiti and a Pakistani national were also injured.
   It was the second reported Iranian attack on Kuwait this month. Tehran
   denies involvement (BBC headline, "Two Italians killed in strike in
   Kuwait, as Iran denies responsibility"); Reuters notes Iran's Foreign
   Ministry did not immediately respond to its request for comment.
   Sources: Reuters, "Two Italians killed in Iranian drone attack in
   Kuwait, Italy says", 26 Nov 2026
   (https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/two-italians-killed-iranian-drone-attack-kuwait-italy-says-2026-11-26/);
   Reuters, "Two Italian workers killed in Iranian strike in Kuwait,
   source says", 26 Nov 2026
   (https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/two-italian-workers-killed-iranian-strike-kuwait-source-says-2026-11-26/).
2. **EU budget talks stall** — EU leaders ended a day of talks in
   Brussels far from a deal on the bloc's nearly €1.6 trillion ($1.75
   trillion) budget proposal for 2028–2034 and on a €45 billion loan for
   Ukraine. Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni said "we're light-years
   away from a solution" and that Italy was "not willing to sign off on a
   budget under which we would have to give more to receive less"; the
   budget needs unanimous approval from all 27 member states. German
   Chancellor Friedrich Merz said leaders would try to reach agreement
   before the end of the year, at a two-day summit starting 17 December,
   and repeated that all 27 members should contribute to aid for Ukraine.
   Sources: Reuters, "Italy's Meloni talks tough on EU budget, warns
   Ukraine on Mediterranean strike", 26 Nov 2026
   (https://www.reuters.com/markets/europe/italys-meloni-talks-tough-eu-budget-warns-ukraine-mediterranean-strike-2026-11-26/);
   Reuters, "EU leaders aim for budget deal before the end of the year,
   Merz says", 26 Nov 2026
   (https://www.reuters.com/world/eu-leaders-aim-budget-deal-before-end-year-merz-says-2026-11-26/).
3. **Jazan refinery hit, one killed** — A hostile aerial projectile hit
   Saudi Aramco's Jazan refinery (400,000 barrels a day) on the Red Sea

   of various nationalities, Saudi Arabia's Energy Ministry said; the
   attack caused a fire, since brought under control, and temporarily
   halted some operations. Reuters notes it was the Houthis' latest blow
   against Saudi facilities, a day after the group claimed attacks on Ras
   Tanura, the kingdom's largest refinery, and the Red Sea ports of
   Jeddah and Jazan. Sources: Al Jazeera, "Attack on Saudi's Jazan
   refinery kills one person, disrupts operations", 26 Nov 2026

4. **Missiles hit Kyiv, couple killed** — Russian ballistic missiles
   struck Kyiv overnight, wounding seven people (two seriously) and

   drone-related sites. In Kharkiv a Shahed drone flew into a
   15th-floor apartment in the Novobavarskyi district and killed a
   married couple, aged 37 and 38, Ukraine's State Emergency Service
   reported. Ukraine reported 113 attack drones launched nationwide;
   local authorities cited ten deaths and 58 injuries across Ukraine in

5. **Coalition one seat short** — New Zealand's ruling centre-right
   coalition narrowly missed a parliamentary majority in the final
   election count, winning 65 seats — one short of a majority in the
   130-seat parliament — leaving Prime Minister Christopher Luxon needing
   the support of the centrist Opportunity Party (seven seats) to remain
   in power. Labour, the largest opposition party, won 36 seats. The
   Electoral Commission said 2,931,604 votes were cast, of which 484,865
   were special votes. Sources: Reuters, "New Zealand's ruling coalition
   falls just short of majority in final election count", 27 Nov 2026
   (https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/new-zealands-ruling-coalition-falls-just-short-majority-final-election-count-2026-11-27/).
